**Featured project – [Portfolio Tracker](https://github.com/eric-delope/portfolio-tracker)** · [▶️ live demo](https://longview-portfolio.streamlit.app)

Long-term portfolio analytics and equity valuation that runs entirely on your own machine: performance against the
market, risk, what you really own inside your ETFs, a guided valuation down to the maximum price worth paying
(incl. reverse DCF), Monte Carlo wealth planning – and a backtest of my own entry strategy, which showed that waiting
for pullbacks mostly cost return on quality stocks. English and German interface, 135 automated tests including
privacy tests.

**Background:** exchange semester in Seoul · internship in project portfolio management (Power BI, Power Platform)

**Toolbox:** Python · pandas · NumPy/SciPy · Streamlit · Plotly · pytest & CI · DCF & valuation · Monte Carlo · Power BI
